About the project

A peaceful coastal community of independent villas

Heavenly Homes is a limited gated community close to the Arabian Sea, combining contemporary villa architecture with quality construction and carefully planned common infrastructure.

Each home is designed for privacy, comfortable family living, and good natural light and ventilation in a coastal setting.

  • Independent villas on 3-cent plots, customisation possible
  • Pillar-beam construction; soil test done before construction
  • Rent out via Airbnb or company lease-back (no returns guaranteed)
  • Easy access to Kochi, Irinjalakuda, Kodungallur and Triprayar

          Common questions

          What are the prices?

          Premium 2-in-1 beach villa: ₹2 crore. Budget single-storey villa: ₹50 lakh, expandable to two storeys. GST, registration and other charges extra unless stated.

          Plots are 3 cents. Customisation is possible.

          Is the project approved?

          Approval and registration details are listed in the Approvals section. Documents are available on request.

          Can I see the villa without travelling?

          Yes. We arrange live video tours, including at Gulf-friendly hours. Use the WhatsApp button or the enquiry form.

          Can I rent out my villa?

          Yes, through Airbnb directly or via company lease-back. Terms on request. Income is not guaranteed.

          Can NRIs buy?

          NRIs can generally buy residential property in India, subject to current FEMA and RBI rules. We assist with the process remotely. Please enquire for details.
